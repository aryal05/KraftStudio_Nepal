## Color Palette
- Primary: Warm brown/tan (#A0826D or similar) for buttons and accents
- Background: Off-white/light gray (#F5F5F5 or #EFEFEF)
- Text: Dark gray/charcoal (#333 or #2C2C2C)
- White: Pure white for header and text overlays
- Accents: Gold/bronze tones for highlights

## Typography
- Headers: Serif font (elegant, editorial style)
- Body: Sans-serif (clean, readable)
- Button text: All caps, sans-serif

## Spacing & Layout
- Generous padding/margins throughout
- Hero section: Content on left ~40%, image on right ~60%
- Category cards: 3-column grid with equal spacing
- Section padding: 60-80px top/bottom
- Container max-width: appears to be around 1200-1400px

## Overall Aesthetic
- Minimalist, luxury, editorial
- Focus on high-quality imagery
- Warm, inviting color scheme
- Professional, high-end furniture store feel
- Lots of whitespace
- Emphasis on photography over graphics
